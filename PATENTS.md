### Publicly disclosed methods

1. **Attribution-patching-to-regulatory-document pipeline** — generating
   structured EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation directly from
   causal circuit analysis of a model's internals, with measured
   faithfulness metrics embedded in the documentation.
2. **CircuitDiff** — version-to-version comparison of decision circuits as
   a CI/CD compliance gate (Article 72 post-market monitoring).
3. **Tamper-evident audit chain** — SHA-256 hash-chained audit records for
   compliance evidence integrity.
