Why now

The deadline is the whole story

From 2 August 2026, any high-risk AI system sold into the EU has to keep Annex IV technical documentation. Skip it and the fine reaches €35M or 7% of global revenue, whichever is larger. Most teams still write this by hand, one model at a time. That is the gap Glassbox closes.

The method

Proof from the model's
own wiring

Glassbox ranks every attention head by how much it actually causes the output, then keeps only the heads you truly need. You get the real circuit, not a tidy story told after the fact.

Discover

Attribution patching scores each head by its causal effect. Three forward passes, no correlation guesswork.

Verify

Sufficiency and comprehensiveness find the smallest circuit that still reproduces the behaviour, checked against full ablation.

Document

Write the full 9-section Annex IV vault. Same input gives the same output, and it is hashed so nobody can edit it quietly.

How it works

From prompt to evidence

The same call works whether you are auditing a loan model, a triage classifier, or a CV screener. It picks its own counterfactual when you let it.

# any prompt, any domain
from glassbox import GlassboxV2, build_annex_iv_vault

gb = GlassboxV2(model)
result = gb.analyze(
    prompt="Income €42,000. Decision:",
    correct=" Approved",
    incorrect=" Denied",
    corruption_strategy="auto",
)
result["faithfulness"]["f1"]   # 0.89
vault = build_annex_iv_vault(result)

EU AI Act

Annex IV, written for you

Annex IV has nine sections. Glassbox fills eight of them straight from the model. The ninth, the EU Declaration of Conformity, is a legal sign-off you keep in your own hands.

§1General description of the system
§2Development process and design
§3Monitoring, functioning, control
§4Performance metrics
§5Risk management (Article 9)
§6Changes across the lifecycle
§7Standards applied (Article 40)
§8Declaration of conformity — you sign
§9Post-market monitoring (Article 72)

A confident answer is not the same as a correct one.

Confidence and circuit faithfulness barely correlate (r = 0.009). That finding is why auditors need the circuit, not the score.
